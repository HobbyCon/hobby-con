FAQ

Do I need to attend both days?

No. You can join either day or both. Each day has its own sign up form and its own ticket.

What is included in my ticket?

Your ticket includes lunch and light beverages, with a lunch of sandwiches, snacks, and light beverages. Light beverages do not include alcohol, but you are welcome to bring your own. You will also receive freebies at check-in. Please note any food allergies or dietary restrictions on the sign up form.

What rules are used and what do I need to bring?

We play by National Mahjong League rules. Please bring your own NMJL 2026 card.

How do the prizes and raffle work?

Prizes are given out at the end of each day. Raffle winners are selected at the end of Sunday. Winners who are not present will be contacted by organizers to get their prizes.

Where is the tournament?

The tournament is in Setauket, NY, on the Long Island Sound. The full address is shared with attendees.
