Brand Outreach & Event Planning Intern

New York, NY (Hybrid) · Unpaid Internship
Starts January 4, 2027 · 8 Weeks · 10 Hours Per Week

The Role

As our Brand Outreach & Event Planning Intern, you will help launch a new brand and take part in meetings with our founders. You will help identify and secure brand partners and plan and execute our events, reporting to one of our founders.

What You Will Do

  • Research prospective brand partners, sponsors and vendors, and draft outreach and pitch materials for review
  • Track outreach and follow-ups, prepare briefing notes for meetings and assist with partnership and sponsorship proposals
  • Support event logistics, including venue and vendor coordination, scheduling and run-of-show documents
  • Maintain event checklists and timelines, and escalate risks to your supervising founder
  • Provide on-site support to partners, vendors and attendees during events
  • Collect partner and vendor feedback after each event and prepare a written debrief

What We Are Looking For

Required

  • Excellent written and verbal communication skills, particularly in business correspondence
  • Strong organizational skills and attention to detail
  • Comfort initiating contact with external parties and responding professionally
  • Ability to meet deadlines and manage multiple priorities

Preferred

  • Prior experience in sales, sponsorship, partnerships, event coordination or a related field
  • Proficiency with spreadsheets and CRM or tracking tools
  • Availability to attend three to five events per month on weeknights and weekends in New York City

What You Will Learn

  • You will help launch a new brand in New York City and take part in meetings with company founders
  • You will gain practical experience in outreach, pitching, and partnership development
  • You will gain experience in event planning, from logistics through on-site execution
  • Our team will train you in the platforms and processes we use to secure brand sponsorships
  • You will receive direct mentorship and feedback from one of our founders
  • Resume building and editing assistance
  • Potential opportunity to grow within the company

What You Will Have Accomplished

By the end of the internship, you will have a documented record of the partners you contacted and growth metrics as a result of community outreach.

How to Apply

Please submit your résumé and a brief cover note.

Content Creation & Social Media Intern

New York, NY (Hybrid) · Unpaid Internship
Starts January 4, 2027 · 8 Weeks · 10 Hours Per Week

The Role

As our Content Creation & Social Media Intern, you will help launch a new brand and take part in meetings with our founders. You will develop and produce photo, video and written content for our channels, and our team will teach you to use essential marketing tools and best practices.

What You Will Do

  • Develop and maintain a content calendar
  • Produce photo, video, and written content for social media platforms including Facebook, Instagram, TikTok and YouTube
  • Contribute to our email campaigns
  • Assist with website maintenance
  • Capture and edit content at our events
  • Deliver three high-quality content drafts each week, along with the edits requested in the previous week's review
  • Submit all content to our team for review before it is published
  • Engage on social platforms with brands and audiences with positive encouragement
  • Remain within compliance of the mission and vision of HobbyCon and all brand guide materials

What We Are Looking For

Required

  • Excellent written and verbal communication skills, including copywriting
  • A personal smartphone for capturing and editing content, using free editing tools such as CapCut and Edits
  • Strong organizational skills and consistent adherence to deadlines
  • Ability to incorporate feedback and revise your work promptly
  • Availability to attend three to five events per month on weeknights and weekends in New York City

Preferred

  • Video editing experience
  • Experience with Canva and the Adobe Suite
  • Familiarity with AI and emerging tools
  • Graphic design experience a plus
  • Claude Code experience a plus

What You Will Learn

  • You will help launch a new brand in New York City and take part in meetings with company founders
  • Our team will train you in essential marketing tools, paid advertising, and organic content growth
  • You will gain hands-on experience across Meta Business Suite, Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, email campaigns and website maintenance
  • You will receive direct mentorship and feedback
  • Resume building and editing assistance
  • Potential opportunity to grow within the company

What You Will Have Accomplished

By the end of the internship, you will have produced a content calendar, captured content at our events and shared your final outputs.

How to Apply

Please submit your résumé and a brief cover note.

Puzzle Library Volunteer

McCarren Park, Brooklyn, NY · Volunteer
One hour of service for each day you stop by

The Role

In July 2026 we installed the first free outdoor puzzle library in New York City at the McCarren Parkhouse in Brooklyn, and we are committed to its ongoing maintenance. Help us keep it stocked and in good shape for the neighborhood.

What You Will Do

  • Stop by the puzzle library and let us know if it needs more puzzles
  • Let us know if the library is in good condition, free from vandalism or weathering

What You Get

An hour of service for each day you stop by and let us know. It is easy volunteer hours.
